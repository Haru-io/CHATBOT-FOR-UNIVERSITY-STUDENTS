TITLE: ABES Campus Life and Facilities
SOURCE: https://www.abes.ac.in/life-at-abes.html

Officially described facilities include a swimming pool, floodlit stadium, badminton/lawn-tennis/basketball/table-tennis courts, air-conditioned classrooms, medical room with ambulance services, auditorium, open-air theatre, seminar halls, canteens, hostels and gym facilities in boys' and girls' hostels.

The page also describes student clubs and activities supporting personal growth, community engagement and creative expression.
