TITLE: ABES Library — Rules and Resources
SOURCE: https://www.abes.ac.in/library.html

Official library rules include:
- Entry is allowed to identity-card holders.
- Users should maintain peace and avoid disturbing readers.
- Smoking, eating and drinking are prohibited in the library premises.
- Reference books, newspapers, magazines and journals should not be taken out.
- Borrowing facilities are available to members.
- Members should verify issued books before leaving the circulation counter.
- Repeated non-return can lead to withdrawal of borrowing facilities for a period decided by the Librarian.

For current timings, fines or borrowing limits, use the latest official library information.
