TITLE: ABES Admissions — 2026-27
SOURCE: https://www.abes.ac.in/courses-offered.html
SOURCE: https://erp.abes.ac.in/onlineregistration/

The official ABES course/admission page states that for 2026-27, 85% seats are filled through counselling based on JEE-2026 and applicable AICTE/AKTU eligibility, while 15% are available for direct admissions according to college policy.

The official ERP instructions state that physical reporting is mandatory to confirm admission and that required fees must be paid according to the applicable schedule.

Admission dates and procedures can change; prefer the latest official notice.
